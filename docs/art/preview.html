SCRIBBIES · PROPOSED ART DIRECTION

Still Hopple. A little more expressive.

A focused refinement of the original bunny. This is a review sample, not a change to the game.

ORIGINAL

The familiar bunny

White fur, purple eyes, pink paws,
round cheeks and long ears.

PROPOSED

The same character, refined

A clearer folded ear, softer outline,
fuller cheeks and warm, simple shading.

Keep: the sweet face, round body, big feet and original colors.
Refine: the silhouette, line consistency and readable expressions. No fur texture or added costume.

Original: unmodified bunny renderer from the legacy game (CSS-based). Proposal: editable SVG. The rest of the collection stays unchanged pending review.
